Meet Kimberly Smith

Kimberly Smith is currently the Vice Principal at a private elementary school in Beaverton, Oregon and has dedicated more than two decades to helping elementary students become confident and successful learners.

Her background in classroom instruction, intervention, learning support, and educational leadership allows her to identify each student's individual strengths and learning needs.

Kimberly specializes in personalized tutoring that helps students strengthen foundational skills while building confidence and independence.
